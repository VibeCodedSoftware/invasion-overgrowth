# Invasion: Overgrowth - Credits

## Original work
All code, models and textures in this mod are original. The Fleshling and Flesh Thing models and the bloody cobblestone,
flesh, hair, Blood Oak leaves and leaf litter textures were made by the mod's author.

## Built from your own game files
The Temple Guardian, Ents, blood, blood geyser, giant saplings, Blood Oak boats and the portal effect are built at runtime
from the player's own installed Minecraft textures, so no Minecraft textures are redistributed. The Overgrowth's
terrain settings (`worldgen/noise_settings/overgrowth.json`) are an adjusted copy of the vanilla Overworld settings, as is usual for
mods and datapacks that add dimensions.

## Inspiration
A spiritual successor to *Hostile Worlds: Invasion* by Corosus. No code or assets from Hostile Worlds or Particle Man are included.
